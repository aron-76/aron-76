### Current Work

I am building **Basehalf**, a compound-thinking workspace where ideas accumulate, connect, and grow.

My current focus is the product and systems layer around:

- Canvas-first knowledge work and graph-based exploration
- AI tool orchestration, context composition, and agent-facing workflows
- Dense React interfaces for writing, mapping, collaboration, and review
- TypeScript service boundaries, Prisma-backed data models, and operational workflows

### Engineering Focus

- Product systems that stay understandable as they scale
- Interfaces that make complex state visible and usable
- Backends with clear contracts, testable boundaries, and pragmatic automation
- AI features that are shaped around real workflows instead of demos
